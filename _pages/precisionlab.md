Genomics has transformed how medicine is practiced and allowed us to identify and treat diseases at the molecular level. We are working on multiple projects that explore this interface and attempt to integrate: multiomics (that includes DNA/RNA/Proteomics/Metabolomics) along with its integration with clinical oncology data to identify personalized biomarkers. We use this emerging framework to develop prognostic and predictive models guiding treatment decisions.
